# Star Growth Plan (5 → 700+ is a marathon, not a commit)

Honest note first: no README change guarantees stars. Stars follow distribution × a genuinely shareable product. v2's interactive commands make outputs more screenshot-able — that's your shareable unit.

## Week 1 — Ship & polish
- Merge v2, tag a GitHub Release ("v2.0 — Cross-examine the Council") with a demo GIF
- Add repo topics: `claude`, `claude-skill`, `claude-ai`, `anthropic`, `ai-agents`, `decision-making`, `prompt-engineering`
- Record a 30-second GIF: question → debate → verdict → "cross-examine the Adversary" → concession. Put it at the top of the README. This single asset matters more than any text.
- Set the social preview image (Settings → Social preview)

## Week 2-3 — Distribution
- Post the GIF + one real verdict to: r/ClaudeAI, r/Anthropic, Hacker News (Show HN), X/Twitter, LinkedIn
- Best hook: a real decision the Council changed your mind on — not "I built a thing"
- Submit to awesome lists: awesome-claude, awesome-claude-skills, awesome-ai-agents (PRs with your repo)
- Answer "how do I get better AI advice" questions on Reddit/X with a Council screenshot (helpful first, link second)

## Ongoing
- Ship one "Famous Decision" per week as a post series (you already have the format)
- "Council of the Week": invite users to submit decisions via Issues; publish the best output
- Respond to every issue/PR within 24h — early contributors become evangelists
- Pin an issue: "What guest persona should join next?" (engagement magnet)

## What NOT to do
- Don't buy or trade stars — GitHub removes them and it can kill the repo's credibility
- Don't spam communities with bare links; every post needs a real artifact

Realistic path: a well-executed Show HN or one viral X thread is 100-500 stars in a week. Without a distribution hit, expect steady 10-30/month from lists and topics.
